# AI Transparency Notice — PRIME.AI
> EU AI Act Article 50 | Effective: 2026-02-20

## 1. AI System Disclosure

**PRIME.AI uses artificial intelligence systems** in the following ways:

### Chat & Assistant (OpenClaw)
- **What it is**: An AI-powered personal assistant using large language models (Claude, Llama)
- **What it does**: Answers questions, performs research, generates documents, automates tasks
- **Important**: Responses are AI-generated and may contain errors. Critical decisions should be verified by a human.

### EU AI Act Compliance Checker
- **What it is**: A rule-based classification tool (not an LLM)
- **What it does**: Classifies AI systems by EU AI Act risk level and generates compliance reports
- **Important**: This tool provides guidance only — it does not constitute legal advice. Consult a qualified attorney for binding compliance decisions.

### Email Communications
- **Disclosure**: Outreach emails from PRIME.AI may be drafted or personalized with AI assistance
- **Human oversight**: All campaigns reviewed before sending

All content generated by PRIME.AI AI systems is labeled as follows:

| Content Type | Label |

## 3. Synthetic Content / Deepfakes

PRIME.AI does **not** generate:
- Synthetic images of real persons
- Deepfake audio or video
- Biometric data

If any synthetic media is generated in the future, it will be clearly labeled per Article 50(2).
